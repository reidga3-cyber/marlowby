What is Marlowby?

A different American city arrives at your door every month.

Each Marlowby box is built around one city. We spend weeks there, meeting the bakers, roasters, potters, and storytellers who give the place its character, and we pack their best work into a single box.

Inside you'll find five to seven local goods, a cultural experience you can enjoy from home, and a postcard with the stories behind every maker. Explore a new city each month, or rediscover your own.

Our mission

Every box sends a city's small businesses new customers in all fifty states.

  • 60%of every box price is paid to the makers inside it, at full retail value.
  • 100%of products keep the maker's own name, label, and story. We never repackage.
  • 1%of every box funds a grant for a small business chosen by that month's city.
